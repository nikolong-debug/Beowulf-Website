PROVEN VICTORIES

Beowulf's reputation comes from the enemies he has faced and defeated.

GRENDEL

Grendel attacks Heorot for years, terrorizing Hrothgar's people. Beowulf travels to Denmark and fights Grendel without weapons, eventually defeating him through his strength and courage.

GRENDEL'S MOTHER

After Grendel's defeat, Grendel's mother attacks Heorot in revenge. Beowulf follows her to her underwater lair and defeats her.

THE DRAGON

Many years later, Beowulf is king when a dragon threatens his people. Even though he is much older, Beowulf chooses to face the dragon and protect his kingdom.

A LEGACY OF HEROISM

Beowulf's victories show his courage, strength, loyalty, and willingness to protect others. His actions are what make him a legendary Anglo-Saxon hero.
