WHY HIRE BEOWULF?

A hero must have more than strength. Beowulf demonstrates many qualities valued by Anglo-Saxon culture.

COURAGE

Beowulf willingly faces dangerous enemies, including Grendel and the dragon.

STRENGTH

Beowulf's extraordinary physical strength helps him defeat powerful opponents.

LOYALTY

Beowulf travels to help Hrothgar and later protects the people he rules as king.

HONOR

Beowulf wants his actions to earn respect and lasting fame.

LEADERSHIP

Beowulf grows from a young warrior into a king responsible for his people.

SELFLESSNESS

Beowulf repeatedly puts himself in danger to protect other people.

PROVEN HEROISM

Beowulf does not simply claim to be a hero. His actions prove his abilities. He defeats Grendel, defeats Grendel's mother, becomes king, and later faces a dragon threatening his people.

These actions demonstrate why Beowulf would be valuable to a community facing a serious threat.
