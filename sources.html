PROJECT PURPOSE

This website was created as a multimedia advertisement presenting Beowulf as a "hero for hire."

The website demonstrates Beowulf's courage, strength, loyalty, honor, leadership, and selflessness while connecting his actions to important Anglo-Saxon heroic values.
