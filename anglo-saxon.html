ANGLO-SAXON VALUES

Beowulf represents many of the qualities that Anglo-Saxon society valued in a hero.

COURAGE

Heroes were expected to face danger bravely. Beowulf repeatedly fights dangerous enemies.

LOYALTY

Beowulf shows loyalty by helping Hrothgar and protecting his own people as king.

FAME & GLORY

Beowulf wants his actions to be remembered. Heroic deeds can create lasting fame.

LEADERSHIP

Beowulf develops from a young warrior into a responsible king.

HONOR

Beowulf cares about keeping his reputation and proving himself through his actions.

GENEROSITY

Kings and leaders were expected to reward loyal warriors and protect their people.

WHY THESE VALUES MATTER

Beowulf is a strong hero because his actions connect to the values of his society. His courage, loyalty, honor, leadership, and desire for fame make him an example of an Anglo-Saxon warrior.

These qualities make Beowulf more than just a strong fighter. They show why he is respected as both a warrior and a leader.
